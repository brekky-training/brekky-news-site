Brekky News

News, one story at a time

See how a story unfolds, not just the latest headline.

Brekky News groups related coverage into one story and shows how it has changed: what happened first, what's new, which sources back it up, and what's still unclear.

How it works

  1. CollectBrekky reads recent articles from a wide range of news sources.
  2. ClusterArticles about the same event are grouped into a single story, so you don't read it five times.
  3. TrackEach meaningful update is added to the story's timeline, with links to the sources that reported it.
  4. BriefYou open a story and get the current summary and what changed since you last looked.

What a story looks like

  1. Mon 07:10
    First reports of the event appear in two outlets.
  2. Mon 12:40
    Official statement confirms the key details.
    Confirmed by 6 sources
  3. Tue 06:55
    New figures released; earlier estimate revised.
    Latest change
